Expected schema & rules
  • order_id: required and unique
  • order_date: real YYYY-MM-DD date
  • product: required text
  • quantity: whole number, 1–1,000,000
  • unit_price: 0–1 billion, max 2 decimals
  • status: paid / pending / refunded

Pending payments trigger a warning. All six fields are required. Additional columns are allowed. Record numbers count non-empty CSV records, including the header; multiline fields can span physical lines.
